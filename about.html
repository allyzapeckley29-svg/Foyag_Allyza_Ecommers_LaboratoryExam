About Our Shop

CAJA PIZZA is Bontoc's first local pizzeria, located along Sub-ang Street, Poblacion, Bontoc, Mountain Province.

We combine classic stone-oven pizza baking with rich local highland flavors, featuring ingredients like authentic Bontoc longganisa. Founded out of a passion for great food and community gathering, we aim to deliver fresh, handcrafted thin-crust pizzas, pastas, and desserts daily from 9:00 AM to 7:30 PM.
